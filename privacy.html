Hybrid Coach – Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Hybrid Coach is a personal, non-commercial project used only by its developer, Leon Braunstein.

What data is accessed

With the account owner's consent, Hybrid Coach requests read-only access to his own Google Health data: sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, VO2 max estimates, exercise sessions, and basic profile and settings information.

How it is used

The data is used only to display training and recovery trends and to plan the account owner's own training. Hybrid Coach never writes to, modifies or deletes Google Health data.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored only on the owner's personal computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by removing the project folder.

Hybrid Coach's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
